## Game flow (Lucas Nov3)
1. Lobby box: all T, Glock only, no damage. Shoot player = request, shoot back = duo (max 2). Broadcast names. Moved to ready platform. !cancel team -> both back to box; !team single -> solo. Avoid chat commands otherwise.
2. After team select: frozen + invisible; keep all 6 DeltaOps characters (D-Wolf, Stinger, Luna, Vyron, Hackclaw, Shepherd). Replace the DeltaOps display box with a numbered list printed in chat; choose using `!characterselect <number>`. DeltaOps API via CSS PluginCapability so raid assigns characters; abilities must respect duos.
3. Cash-in !cashout from SSG: max $15,000 into game per raid. Start $0 otherwise.
4. Buy phase 30s: weapons, armor, helmet. Vanilla prices (client menu can't show custom prices; custom pricing flag off). Chat shows charge.
5. Ammo: mag price = weapon price/20 (Deagle 700 -> 35, G3SG1 -> 250). Reload deducts money.
6. 5s countdown -> teleport teams to random spawn coords (Lucas sends), frozen 10s, then go.
7. Teams: all T + teammates-are-enemies; plugin zeroes duo damage; vanilla wallbangs. Partner-only glow + name + HP tags.
8. Death: armor vanishes; drop guns/grenades/utility + money bag with their cash. No auto pickup; press E: bag cash added, weapons/utility instantly sold to balance. Ragdolls client-side, not lootable. Duo dead -> spectate partner; whole team dead -> noclip invisible, can't interact, until end.
9. Spaceport loot containers (Lucas Nov6, confirmed design, NOT implemented): next build uses DF-only full transforms and 27 approved model types, with 54 CLOSED/OPENED static paths as the target, not all verified. Select the cross-variant union of 13 big safes, 8 small safes and other DF containers whose counts remain provisional. Hold E (+use) for 5 seconds on normal containers, 10 seconds on small safes and 17 seconds on big safes. Show progress; release or moving away cancels. Atomic completion awards at most once and switches CLOSED to OPENED; the container remains visible and nonlootable for the rest of the raid, without animation playback. Raid-scoped authoritative collected ids/state survive reconnect/late join; a new raid resets eligible placements to CLOSED and collectible. Collision behavior remains undecided; preserve existing intent pending explicit choice and both-state physics QA. Missing genuine OPENED assets block that model, never invent a pose or fall back to collection despawn. This SUPERSEDES the Nov5 collection-despawn rule and historical web239/22-model/global+90-yaw plan, plus the older mini-safe5s/big-safe10s/Hammer-targetname draft. Reward/probability policy and separate item/death-bag rules are unchanged. Historical draft payouts (mini-safe $300-$3,000, big-safe $1,000-$5,000 with per-name spawn rates) remain prior draft context, not verified payouts for the current manifest. Administrative forced despawn/disable and owned lifecycle ClearLoot remain valid. Computer/laptop semantic LOOT TYPES are USER-EXCLUDED by Lucas on Nov6: do not export or place them, and mark manifest/count entries USER-EXCLUDED rather than missing-coverage blockers. The approved target remains 27 models / 54 static-state paths, not a 28th model. Resolve authoritative type/class ids before applying the exclusion; flag ambiguous identities. This does not exclude static world-decor computers, screens or desks, nor automatically exclude computer bags. Existing outputs are immutable; do not delete them. See [SPACEPORT_LOOT_PLAN.md](SPACEPORT_LOOT_PLAN.md) for state, source and acceptance requirements.
10. Match max 30 min; alive & not extracted at 30 min die, lose all.
11. Extraction: 4 physical levers (Ocean coords or map entities, press E); any 2 pulled (any time apart) -> 5 min countdown; at end, players inside extraction zone extract. Levers reset after; reusable. Extraction zone = invisible touch-based trigger entity if Ocean adds one; otherwise a plugin-defined zone.
12. On extract: guns, unused grenades, pistols refunded into SSG balance; armor/helmet NOT. Money cap: CS2 likely $100k (some refs 65,535) untested; revisit 65,535 HUD clamp.
